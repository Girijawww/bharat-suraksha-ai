# Privacy and safety

- The app does not create accounts or intentionally store submitted message text.
- Submitted text or OCR output is processed in memory for the response. Hosting providers may have their own logs; review the host's policy before deployment.
- Never submit real OTPs, PINs, passwords, card details, identity documents, or private conversations.
- URLs are never fetched or opened by this application and are displayed in defanged form.
- This prototype does not prove whether a message is fraudulent or safe.
- Before real-world use, arrange a privacy/security review and native-speaker review of Tamil and Hindi translations.
